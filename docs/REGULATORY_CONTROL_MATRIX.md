# Regulatory control matrix

This matrix maps engineering evidence to control themes. It is not a legal
opinion or a declaration of compliance.

| Theme | MuleCatch evidence | Remaining institutional evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Customer acceptance and KYC risk | Onboarding assessment, reason codes, stored decisions | Approved KYC policy and customer segmentation |
| Mule-account monitoring | Behavioral, temporal, device, graph, and transaction signals | Validated scenarios and thresholds |
| Ongoing enhanced monitoring | Kafka ingest, versioned alerts, feedback/drift modules | Monitoring frequency and review policy |
| Sanctions screening | UNSC ingestion, name/identifier matching, persisted results | Approved sources, refresh SLA, match disposition |
| Explainability | Reasons, feature contributions, subgraphs, narratives | Investigator training and validation |
| Auditability | RBAC audit events and verifiable hash chain | WORM export, retention, access reviews |
| Fraud governance/EWS | Alerts, escalation, KPI and investigation workflow | Board-approved framework and committee reporting |
| STR preparation | Draft, approval, PDF and lifecycle states | FINGate acceptance, credentials, filing SOP |
| Security controls | Auth, rate limits, headers, strict startup/readiness | Bank IAM, SOC monitoring, pen test, DR evidence |
| Customer protection | Human-review design | Notice, grievance, freeze/unfreeze and remediation SOP |

Every deployment must attach evidence references, control owner, reviewer,
review date, and next review date.
